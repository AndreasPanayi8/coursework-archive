# Coursework & Research Archive

A curated archive of coursework, presentations, lecture notes, code, and research material collected across my studies in mathematics and theoretical computer science at the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA) and the National & Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA).

The repository is browsable as a website — the [personal webpage](https://andreaspanayi8.github.io/coursework-archive/) renders this archive with search, a folder tree, and inline previews of PDFs, code, and slides.

## Contents

- **Archimedes Research Unit** — research material and code implementations from my internship at the Archimedes Research Unit, Athena Research Center.
- **Exercises and Labs by Course** — problem sets, solutions, and lab work organized by course, including Advanced AI, Algorithmic Data Science, Algorithms, Approximation Algorithms, Blockchain Foundations, Combinatorial Optimization, Computational Complexity, and Graph Theory.
- **Presentations** — slide decks and supporting files from seminar and course talks, on topics such as communication complexity, temporal graphs, max-flow (Goldberg–Rao), lossy kernelization, smoothed analysis, and CSP inapproximability.
- **LaTeX Intro and Template** — a short introduction to (Xe)LaTeX along with reusable templates.
- **Thesis** — material related to my MSc thesis, *Faster Even-Cycle Listing via Unbalanced Supersaturation*.
- **CV** — academic and technical versions of my curriculum vitae.

## About

I'm a theoretical computer science graduate student working on fine-grained and parameterized algorithms, dynamic and geometric data structures, and extremal graph theory, with applied interests in data management, big data, and machine learning.
