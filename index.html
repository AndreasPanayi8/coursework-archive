01 — Profile

Integrating theoretical research and systems engineering.

I hold a BSc in Mathematics from the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and an M.S. in Theoretical Computer Science (ALMA) jointly offered by the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and the National Technical University of Athens.

My coursework spans both theoretical and applied domains. On the theoretical side, I focus on advanced algorithms and data structures, graph theory, and computational complexity. On the applied side, I have studied supervised and unsupervised machine learning, algorithmic data science, and blockchain foundations.

My current research focuses on fine-grained and parameterized algorithms, dynamic and geometric data structures, and extremal graph theory. On the applied side, my research interests are in data management, big data, machine learning, and algorithmic applications.

05 — Publications

Selected research output.

Listing Even Cycles Faster than the Submodular-Width Barrier
Vasileios Nakos, Hung Q. Ngo, Andreas Panayi
arXiv preprint · Submitted to SIGMOD/PODS
Introduces a novel unbalanced supersaturation approach to enumerate even cycles faster than permitted by the submodular-width barrier, yielding optimal output-sensitive algorithms for a broad class of graph queries. This connects graph algorithms with database-theoretic enumeration beyond the submodular-width barrier. View on arXiv →
